The Story

From Routes to Retention.

I didn't start in marketing. I started on the ground — untangling delivery routes, chasing down late shipments, and building the SOPs that held a fruit-and-vegetable supply chain together across ten thousand daily deliveries.

"Every broken delivery route taught me the same lesson a broken funnel would, later: find where it fails, fix one thing, measure what happens."

That work trained an instinct — spot the pattern, isolate the variable, measure the outcome — and pointed it, almost by accident, at a different kind of exception: the customer who nearly churned, and came back anyway.

Today I build growth systems instead of operational ones. Same instincts, aimed at subscriptions, cohorts, and lifecycle journeys instead of shipments — with the analytical rigor of someone who once had to explain, in a dashboard, exactly why a delivery was late.
