About Me

Building Growth Through Systems, Data & Customer Understanding.

My professional journey began in supply chain and operations, where solving real-world logistics challenges taught me how scalable systems are built.

Working closely with operations, analytics and cross-functional teams gradually led me into performance marketing. Today I focus on customer acquisition, lifecycle marketing, CRM automation, retention and experimentation while bringing an operations-first mindset to every campaign.

I believe sustainable growth comes from understanding customer behaviour, measuring everything and continuously improving processes—not simply increasing marketing spend.
